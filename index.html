How to play

  • Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of the yard.
  • Choose one of the highlighted pawns to move.
  • Land on an opponent to send them back, unless the square is safe.
  • Reach home with an exact roll. A 6, capture, or finish earns another turn.
  • Two pawns together form a blockade; other players cannot pass it.
  • Three consecutive sixes end your turn.
